### 历史补录

本时段集中推进《保护野生动物迁徙物种公约》（CMS）COP12现行决议目录，核验并登记10项独立治理文件：

1. [Resolution 12.1 — Presidency of the Conference of the Parties](https://www.cms.int/document/presidency-conference-parties)：确立主席国在届间期的政治领导、协调和报告职责。
2. [Resolution 12.3 — Manila Declaration on Sustainable Development and Migratory Species](https://www.cms.int/document/manila-declaration-sustainable-development-and-migratory-species)：把迁徙物种保护纳入2030议程、国家规划和跨部门政策。
3. [Resolution 12.4 (Rev.COP14) — Scientific Council](https://www.cms.int/document/scientific-council-1)：更新科学理事会及其会期委员会的治理安排；正式PDF直链本次访问受限，保留待补核。
4. [Resolution 12.5 (Rev.COP14) — National Reports](https://www.cms.int/document/national-reports-6)：规范国家报告时点、格式、数据和执行评估。
5. [Resolution 12.6 (Rev.COP15) — Wildlife Health](https://www.cms.int/document/wildlife-health-2)：以“同一健康”连接生态系统、野生动物、家畜和人类健康，强化污染、生物安全和监测协同。
6. [Resolution 12.8 — Implementation of Articles IV and V](https://www.cms.int/document/implementation-articles-iv-and-v-convention)：统一CMS协定及合作文书的设计、管理和新提案评估标准。
7. [Resolution 12.9 — Review Mechanism and National Legislation Programme](https://www.cms.int/document/establishment-review-mechanism-and-national-legislation-programme)：建立支持性履约审查和国家立法衔接机制。
8. [Resolution 12.10 (Rev.COP14) — Conservation of African-Eurasian Vultures](https://www.cms.int/document/conservation-african-eurasian-vultures-2)：实施2017—2029多物种行动计划，重点控制中毒、能源设施和非法贸易风险。
9. [Resolution 12.11 (Rev.COP15) — Flyways](https://www.cms.int/document/flyways-13)：整合全球迁飞路线行动计划、关键栖息地保护和区域协作。
10. [Resolution 12.12 (Rev.COP15) — Action Plans for Birds](https://www.cms.int/document/action-plans-birds-12)：规范鸟类行动计划的实施、筹资、监测和定期审查。
